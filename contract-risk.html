AI Recommendation: Negotiate for a 3-year term with two 1-year renewal options to reduce long-term commitment risk.
ARTICLE 2: STAFFING REQUIREMENTS FTE Count: 18 employees (22% below industry standard) Historical data indicates this will lead to 18% overtime costs and potential service level penalties.
AI Recommendation: Increase FTE count to 22 and include a staffing adjustment clause tied to actual service demand metrics.
ARTICLE 3: PRICING AND ADJUSTMENTS Base Year Pricing: Fixed Years 2-5: No annual inflation adjustment specified Food cost inflation averages 3.8% annually, creating 4-6% margin erosion risk each year.
AI Recommendation: Add annual price adjustment clause tied to Consumer Price Index (CPI) with a 3% cap.
ARTICLE 4: SCOPE OF SERVICES Core Services: Daily cafeteria operations, cleaning services Special Events: "As requested by client" (Vague language) This creates potential for 15-20% scope creep.
AI Recommendation: Define special events scope explicitly with volume limits and separate pricing schedule.
ARTICLE 5: PAYMENT TERMS Payment Schedule: Net 30 days (Standard terms)
AI Recommendation: Consider requesting 50% advance payment for special events to improve cash flow.
ARTICLE 6: PERFORMANCE METRICS Service Level Agreements: Standard corporate requirements Penalty Structure: 2% of monthly fee for missed KPIs
AI Recommendation: Add performance bonus clause for exceeding KPIs by 10% or more to balance penalty structure.
RISK SUMMARY: - Labor assumptions: $1.4M financial impact risk - Inflation protection gap: $450K potential impact - Scope definition issues: $250K potential impact - Payment terms: Low risk
